    Questions, answered

    Before your session.

    Why do I need to upload five photos?

    Multiple angles help us understand facial structure, proportions, expressions and the way light behaves across your features. This produces a more stable and recognizable identity than relying on a single image.

    Why is a consultation required?

    Because likeness is subjective. The consultation lets you tell us which features, expressions and visual qualities are essential, so the final portraits remain aligned with how you see yourself.

    Is Lumora just another AI image generator?

    No. Lumora is a human-guided portrait service. AI is one part of the production process, supported by identity review, style direction and final quality control.

    Can I choose a luxury editorial reference?

    Yes. You can select one of our curated styles or describe the visual mood you want. Our specialist will recommend the closest direction and explain what will work best with your reference photos.
